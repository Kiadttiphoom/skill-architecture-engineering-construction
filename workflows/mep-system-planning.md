# Building MEP Systems Planning Workflow

## Step-by-Step Engineering Execution
1. **Electrical Power & Life Safety [S-008, S-017]:**
   - Service sizing for utility supply (MEA / PEA); Main Distribution Board (MDB) layout.
   - 20-second automatic standby emergency generator installation for critical life safety loads.
   - Enforce Building Energy Code (BEC) Lighting Power Density (LPD) limits.
2. **Sanitary & Water Supply [S-009]:**
   - Segregate soil, waste, stormwater, and grease drainage networks.
   - Size domestic potable water storage for a minimum 24-hour autonomous reserve.
   - Integrate grease traps and aerobic septic treatment tanks.
3. **HVAC & Ventilation Systems [S-008]:**
   - Size cooling loads and specify high-efficiency equipment; ensure unwindowed restrooms receive $\ge 4	ext{ ACH}$.
